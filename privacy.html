RealmKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

RealmKit is a browser extension that provides additional tools and utilities for supported game pages.

Data Collection

RealmKit does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

Local Storage

RealmKit uses the browser's local extension storage to save extension preferences, such as whether the extension is enabled. This information remains stored locally in the user's browser.

Website Access

RealmKit operates on supported Imperia Online pages and requires access to those pages in order to provide its functionality, including map search, navigation, and game management tools.

Third-Party Services

RealmKit does not send personal information to external analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RealmKit's functionality changes. Any updates will be published on this page.
